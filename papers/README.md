# Manuscript ideas derived from the thesis

This folder contains preliminary manuscript ideas derived from the thesis project.
These are not final papers, submission-ready manuscripts, or claims of completed
scientific validation. They are working documents for organizing possible
publication paths, figures, arguments, limitations, and next steps.

The current thesis evidence suggests two possible manuscript directions:

1. `paper_1_latent_geometry`: a geometric audit of the AntigenLM latent space.
   This paper would focus on molecular metric preservation, effective/intrinsic
   dimension, local temporal neighborhoods, and methodological cautions about
   using latent spaces for continuous dynamics.

2. `paper_2_latent_dynamics`: subtype-dependent probabilistic dynamics in PCA
   space. This paper would focus on monthly latent centroids, rolling-origin
   evaluation, minimal Gaussian dynamics, calibration, and the difference
   between H1N1 and H3N2.

Both directions should remain explicitly preliminary until the relevant
robustness checks, baselines, and prospective validation protocols are complete.
